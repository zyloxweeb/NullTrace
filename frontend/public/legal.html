Privacy Policy

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Giuseppe Fattor, known online as zylox, reachable at the contact address below. NullTrace is a personal, non-commercial project and is not operated by a company.

What data we process

When you upload a file for analysis, NullTrace processes the following:

  • The file itself — used exclusively for static analysis (hashing, string extraction, IOC detection, pattern matching). It is never opened for reading content beyond what the analyzer requires.
  • Technical metadata derived from the file: SHA-256, MD5, MIME type, file size, entropy value, extracted strings, IOCs, suspicious patterns.
  • Your IP address — collected automatically by our hosting provider (Vercel) as part of standard HTTP request logs. We do not access or store these logs ourselves.
Important: NullTrace does not collect your name, email, identity, or any other personal information. No account is required to use the service.

How long we keep your data

Uploaded files are never stored on disk. They are read into memory, analyzed, and immediately discarded.

Analysis results (metadata derived from the file: hashes, MIME type, extracted strings, IOCs, patterns, verdict) are temporarily stored on our server as a JSON file to enable the session cache — this avoids re-analyzing the same file twice. These result files are automatically deleted after 24 hours. No manual intervention is required; deletion is enforced at the infrastructure level on every new analysis request.

After 24 hours, no trace of your file or its analysis remains on our systems.

Server access logs (including IP addresses) are managed by Vercel according to their own retention policy, which is outside our control. You can review Vercel's privacy policy at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Legal basis for processing (GDPR Art. 6)

The processing of your file and its derived metadata is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — processing necessary for the performance of the service you explicitly requested by uploading the file. No processing occurs without your active action.

Third-party services

NullTrace is deployed on Vercel Inc. (San Francisco, CA, USA), which acts as a data processor under a standard Data Processing Agreement. Vercel may temporarily process your IP and request data in accordance with their own privacy policy. No other third-party services are used. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no social media integrations.

Data transfers outside the EU

Vercel's infrastructure may route or process requests through servers located in the United States. This transfer is covered by Vercel's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with the European Commission, as required by GDPR Chapter V.

Your rights under GDPR

As a user in the European Union, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access — you can request information on what data we hold about you.
  • Right to erasure — you can request deletion of any data associated with you.
  • Right to restriction — you can request we limit processing of your data.
  • Right to object — you can object to processing at any time.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — you can file a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali (garanteprivacy.it).

Since we do not collect identifying information, exercising some rights may be limited in practice — we cannot identify which server logs correspond to you without additional information you provide.

Cookie Policy

Our cookie usage

NullTrace does not use cookies for tracking, analytics, advertising, or any other purpose. No first-party cookies are set by this application.

TL;DR: We don't use cookies. We don't track you. We don't know who you are.

Third-party and infrastructure cookies

Our hosting provider, Vercel, may set technical cookies or use similar storage mechanisms as part of their infrastructure (e.g., for routing or security). These are outside our control and are covered by Vercel's own Cookie Policy.

Your browser itself may store data locally (e.g., cached assets). This is standard browser behavior and does not constitute tracking.

Local storage

NullTrace may use browser localStorage or sessionStorage to temporarily retain analysis state during your session (e.g., last loaded result). This data:

  • Never leaves your browser.
  • Is not transmitted to any server.
  • Is cleared when you close your browser tab or clear browser data.

Your choices

You can clear any locally stored data at any time through your browser settings. Since we do not set tracking cookies, there is no cookie consent banner required — and we intend to keep it that way.
